## Why the other companies were not selected

### 1) Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. — Not selected
AMD is a very strong company and an increasingly credible AI challenger, but it was not the best choice here.

#### Why it is attractive
- It has gained meaningful recognition as the leading challenger to NVIDIA in AI accelerators.
- Reuters reported on **2026-09-21** that AMD joined the **$1 trillion market-cap club**, showing how sharply investor confidence has improved.
- It has a diversified business across data center, client, gaming, and embedded segments.

#### Why it was not chosen
- **Valuation and expectations are now very elevated**
  - After the rerating, a lot of future success is already reflected in the stock price.
- **Execution risk is still high**
  - AMD must prove that its MI-series and future AI platforms can sustain share gains, margins, and software adoption.
- **It is still the challenger, not the leader**
  - NVIDIA’s ecosystem and platform maturity remain superior.
  - AMD is compelling, but the burden of proof is higher.

#### Bottom line on AMD
AMD is a strong high-growth candidate, but compared with NVIDIA it offers **less certainty** and **more execution risk** at a time when the market has already priced in significant optimism.

### 2) Nebius Group N.V. — Not selected
Nebius is interesting and potentially highly asymmetric, but it is not the best investment choice for a primary recommendation.

#### Why it is attractive
- It is emerging as a specialized AI infrastructure provider.
- It has shown rapid growth and strategic validation.

  - A long-term AI infrastructure supply agreement with Meta worth up to approximately **$27 billion**

- **Greater customer concentration risk**
  - Large strategic contracts help, but they also create dependence on a limited number of relationships.
- **Less proven business model**
  - Nebius is still earlier in commercialization than NVIDIA or even AMD.
- **More volatile and less durable**
  - The upside can be large, but the path is less certain and more dependent on successful scaling and funding access.

#### Bottom line on Nebius
Nebius may offer outsized upside for aggressive investors, but it is much riskier and less proven than NVIDIA. For a best-in-class investment recommendation, it does not surpass NVIDIA’s combination of leadership and reliability.
