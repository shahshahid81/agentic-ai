I strongly support the motion that there should be strict laws to regulate LLMs.

LLMs are not harmless tools; they are powerful systems that can influence what people believe, how they act, and even how societies function. Without strict laws, we leave the public exposed to major risks: misinformation at scale, manipulation, privacy violations, copyright abuse, discrimination, and unsafe automation in critical areas like education, healthcare, hiring, and law.

First, LLMs can generate convincing falsehoods instantly and cheaply. That means one actor can flood the internet with fake news, scam messages, impersonation, and propaganda far more efficiently than ever before. In a world already struggling with trust, unregulated LLMs make deception faster, cheaper, and harder to detect.

Second, these systems learn from massive datasets, often collected without meaningful consent. That raises serious privacy and ownership concerns. If companies can scrape personal data, creative work, and sensitive information without accountability, then innovation is being built on exploitation.

Third, LLMs can reproduce and amplify bias. If they are used in hiring, education, policing, or healthcare without strict oversight, they may reinforce discrimination while hiding it behind the appearance of objectivity. That is dangerous because people tend to trust machine outputs too easily.

Fourth, the economic power of LLMs is too large to be left to voluntary self-regulation. Companies have incentives to move fast, capture market share, and cut corners. History shows that when powerful industries regulate themselves, the public usually pays the price. Strict laws create clear boundaries, enforce transparency, and ensure accountability.

This motion is not anti-innovation. It is pro-responsibility. Strict regulation does not ban LLMs; it makes them safer, fairer, and more trustworthy. We regulate cars, medicines, food, and aircraft because their benefits are enormous but so are their risks. LLMs now belong in that category.

If we want AI to serve society instead of endangering it, strict laws are not optional. They are necessary.
