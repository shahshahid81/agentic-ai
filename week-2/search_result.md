In 2026, the most commonly discussed AI agent frameworks are LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, Microsoft Agent Framework, and OpenAI’s Agents SDK/Swarm lineage, with many comparisons also including Mastra, PydanticAI, and Semantic Kernel. Recent 2026 landscape writeups consistently describe the market as having “consolidated” around a few major options, rather than dozens of equally popular tools. ([socialcrawl.dev](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/ai-agent-frameworks-2026-developer-field-guide?utm_source=openai))

A practical summary of the field is: LangGraph is often favored for orchestration and production workflows; CrewAI for role-based multi-agent setups; AutoGen/Microsoft Agent Framework for research-heavy multi-agent collaboration; and OpenAI’s Agents SDK for simpler, vendor-native agent builds. Enterprise trend reports and framework surveys also emphasize that standards like MCP are becoming important alongside the frameworks themselves, because teams increasingly want interoperable agents, tools, and context sharing. ([langchain.com](https://www.langchain.com/state-of-agent-engineering?utm_source=openai))
