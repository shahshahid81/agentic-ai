# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| Approx. starting price | About $41,630 | About $37,795–$39,995 |
| EPA range | Up to about 321 miles (varies by trim) | Roughly 250–320 miles (varies by trim) |
| Cargo space | About 72 cu ft with rear seats folded | 29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; 59.7 cu ft folded |
| Charging advantage | Tesla Supercharger access | Ford BlueOval Charge Network; up to 150 kW DC fast charging |
| Driver assist | Autopilot / Full Self-Driving (Supervised) | BlueCruise available |

## Fleet takeaways

**Tesla Model Y**
- Stronger charging-network advantage thanks to Supercharger access.
- Best-in-class cargo room for sales use cases with samples, bags, or demo materials.
- Higher starting price, but likely the easiest choice for minimizing charging friction.

**Ford Mustang Mach-E**
- Lower entry price and still offers competitive range.
- Good cargo space, though meaningfully less than the Model Y.
- BlueCruise is a useful highway-assist feature for sales reps, but charging convenience depends more on third-party networks.

## Recommendation

For a 100-car sales fleet, **recommend the Tesla Model Y** if charging simplicity, range confidence, and cargo volume are the top priorities. The Supercharger network and larger cargo capacity make it the stronger operational fit for high-usage field teams.

Choose the **Ford Mustang Mach-E** only if lower upfront cost is more important than charging-network convenience and maximum cargo space.
