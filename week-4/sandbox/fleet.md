# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick Comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starting price | ~$39,990–$41,630 | ~$36,495 MSRP |
| EPA range | ~260–357 miles | ~230–320 miles |
| Seating | 5; some 7-seat versions | 5 |
| Cargo space | 33.1 cu ft | 29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; up to 59.7 cu ft folded |
| DC fast charging | Up to ~250 kW peak | Up to ~150 kW peak |
| Charging network | Tesla Supercharger access, native NACS | BlueOval Charge Network + public charging |

## Fleet Takeaways

**Tesla Model Y**
- Best for reps who drive high mileage and need the strongest charging network experience.
- Strong range and fast charging reduce downtime on the road.
- Potential downsides are a slightly less conventional user interface and a higher-friction service/repair experience for some fleet operators.

**Ford Mustang Mach-E**
- More traditional for drivers used to Ford products and may be easier to onboard.
- Good cargo flexibility and solid daily-range capability for route-based sales work.
- Charging speed and range are generally a step behind the Model Y, which matters for heavy road use.

## Recommendation

**Choose the Tesla Model Y** for the 100-car sales fleet if charging convenience, route flexibility, and minimizing downtime are the top priorities. Its Supercharger access and stronger real-world charging experience make it the better fit for a mobile sales team.

**Choose the Ford Mustang Mach-E** only if the company places a higher value on traditional dealer/service familiarity and wants to stay within the Ford ecosystem, accepting somewhat weaker charging performance.
